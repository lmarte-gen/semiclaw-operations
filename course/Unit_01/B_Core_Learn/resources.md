# Unit 1: Foundations & Ecosystem - Resources

## Essential Reading & Core Assets (Domain & Financial Fluency)
- **TSMC Annual Report (20-F):** Essential reading for understanding foundry operations, wafer sales, and capacity planning.
- **ASML Investor Day Presentations:** Detailed breakdown of EUV Lithography monopolies and tool sales.
- **SEC Filings (10-K, 10-Q):** NVDA (Fabless/Compute), TSM (Foundry), ASML (Equipment), ARM (IP Licensing).
- **CFA Institute Textbooks:** Level 1 & 2 FSA (Financial Statement Analysis) focusing on Capex, Depreciation Schedules, and Free Cash Flow (FCF).

## Books & Industry Sub-stacks
- **Chip War: The Fight for the World's Most Critical Technology** by Chris Miller
  - *Why:* Excellent macro overview for global supply chain mapping.
- **SemiAnalysis (Dylan Patel)**
  - *Why:* Deep dives into semiconductor cyclicality, bullwhips, and bottlenecks.

## Agentic Build Track (Tools & Docs)
- **JSON Schema Documentation:** [JSON Schema](https://json-schema.org/) for defining strict output structures (`company.json`).
- **OpenClaw Documentation:** Guides on building tools and AgentSkills for local orchestration.
- **Model API Docs:** OpenAI or Anthropic documentation on Structured Outputs and strict JSON mode.
- **Python Libraries:** `PyPDF2` or `pdfplumber` for text extraction from dense SEC filing PDFs before passing to the LLM.

## Synthesis & Content Flywheel
- **Writing Frameworks:** Ship 30 for 30 or similar guides on structuring high-engagement essays and X (Twitter) threads.
