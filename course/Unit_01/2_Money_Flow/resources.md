# Module 2: The Money Flow (Financial Fluency Track) - Resources

## Core Assets: Financial Filings
We map the global supply chain not just by tech, but by value capture. These filings are critical to understanding how Capex, Depreciation, and Gross Margins dictate Moats.

- **NVIDIA (NVDA) - 10-K (Fabless/Compute):**
  - *Link:* [NVDA 2026 10-K Filing (SEC EDGAR)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581026000021/nvda-20260125.htm)
  - *Why:* Analyze software-like gross margins from a hardware designer.
  
- **Taiwan Semiconductor (TSM) - 20-F (Foundry):**
  - *Link:* [TSMC 2025 20-F Filing (SEC EDGAR)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1046179/000119312525083423/d896993d20f.htm)
  - *Why:* Analyze Capex intensity (~$20B+ per fab) and Wafer Sales revenue models.
