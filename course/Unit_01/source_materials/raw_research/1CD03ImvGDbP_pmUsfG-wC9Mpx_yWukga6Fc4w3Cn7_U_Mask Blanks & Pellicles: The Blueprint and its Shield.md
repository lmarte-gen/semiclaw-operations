# Mask Blanks & Pellicles: The Blueprint and its Shield

The "mask" is the master template or blueprint of the circuit design that the light is projected through.

* **Mask Blanks:** This is the ultra-pure, perfectly flat piece of quartz that the blueprint is etched onto. If the blank from the supplier has even a single microscopic defect in it, that flaw will be printed onto **every single chip** on the wafer. This is called a **repeating defect** and is catastrophic for yield.  
* **Pellicles:** This is an extremely thin, transparent membrane stretched over the mask. Its only job is to act as a dust cover. A single speck of dust on the mask would block the light and create a fatal defect on every chip.  
* **How Suppliers Help** For mask blanks, the goal is perfection—zero native defects. For pellicles, the challenge is two-fold:  
  1. **Transmission:** The pellicle must let almost 100% of the EUV light pass through. If it absorbs any light, the dose reaching the wafer is reduced, which can increase those noisy stochastic defects.  
  2. **Thermal Stability:** The intense EUV light heats the pellicle. If it warps or expands even slightly from the heat, it distorts the projected pattern, causing layers to be misaligned (**overlay errors**).
